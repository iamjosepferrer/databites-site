About

A practitioner who shows his work

I'm a data scientist and educator from Barcelona, based in Rotterdam. I help institutions and research organisations make decisions with their own data, through data visualization, analytics and GenAI.

I studied Engineering Physics (UPC) and a master in Intelligent Interactive Systems (UPF). Before going independent I was an analytics engineer in the Generalitat de Catalunya's tourism data unit. Every weekday I publish a map or data story built on public data.
